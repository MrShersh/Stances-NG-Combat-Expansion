$SNGECE_Title	Stances NG - Combat Expansion
$SNGECE_NotLoaded	Stances NG - Combat Expansion.esp is not active. Enable it in the load order.
$SNGECE_Header_Bonuses	Stance Bonuses
$SNGECE_Enabled	Enable stance bonuses
$SNGECE_Enabled_Tip	Buffs and debuffs for the Bear, Wolf and Hawk stances. Neutral stance has no modifiers.
$SNGECE_MeleeOnly	Only with melee weapons
$SNGECE_MeleeOnly_Tip	Stance bonuses and penalties work only while a melee weapon, a shield or bare fists are in hand. With a bow, a crossbow, a staff or only spells they are off. Spells are never affected by stances.
$SNGECE_Stance_Bear	Bear
$SNGECE_Stance_Bear_Desc	Bear stance emphasizes aggression: more attack and break power than any other stance, at the cost of defence, maneuverability and stamina regeneration.
$SNGECE_Stance_Wolf	Wolf
$SNGECE_Stance_Wolf_Desc	Wolf stance is the most defensive: better blocking, less damage taken and harder to stagger, with a modest loss of attack power.
$SNGECE_Stance_Hawk	Hawk
$SNGECE_Stance_Hawk_Desc	Hawk stance focuses on evasive movement: faster but weaker attacks that tire you less, with better stamina regeneration.
$SNGECE_Stance_Neutral	Neutral
$SNGECE_AttackDamageBuff	Attack Damage Buff
$SNGECE_AttackDamageBuff_Tip	Attack damage is increased by this amount.
$SNGECE_PowerAttackDamageBuff	Power Attack Damage Buff
$SNGECE_PowerAttackDamageBuff_Tip	Power attack damage is increased by this amount.
$SNGECE_IncomingDamageDebuff	Damage Taken Increase
$SNGECE_IncomingDamageDebuff_Tip	All physical damage you take in this stance, blocked or not, is higher by this amount.
$SNGECE_AttackSpeedDebuff	Attack Speed Debuff
$SNGECE_AttackSpeedDebuff_Tip	Attack speed is decreased by this amount, up to 90 (at 100 the engine would treat attack speed as normal again). 0 turns the effect off.
$SNGECE_MoveSpeedDebuff	Movement Speed Debuff
$SNGECE_MoveSpeedDebuff_Tip	Movement speed is decreased by this many points, up to 90.
$SNGECE_StaminaRegenDebuff	Stamina Regeneration Debuff
$SNGECE_StaminaRegenDebuff_Tip	Stamina regenerates slower by this amount.
$SNGECE_AttackDamageDebuff	Attack Damage Debuff
$SNGECE_AttackDamageDebuff_Tip	Attack damage is decreased by this amount, up to 90.
$SNGECE_BlockBuff	Block Efficiency
$SNGECE_BlockBuff_Tip	Only while blocking: the share of damage your block stops grows by this amount. Example: a block that stops 50 percent stops 55 at +10, 60 at +20.
$SNGECE_StaggerBuff	Stagger Reduction Buff
$SNGECE_StaggerBuff_Tip	You are this much harder to stagger.
$SNGECE_DamageResistBuff	Damage Taken Reduction
$SNGECE_DamageResistBuff_Tip	All physical damage you take in this stance, blocked or not, is lower by this amount. Works on top of blocking.
$SNGECE_PowerAttackStaminaBuff	Power Attack Stamina Buff
$SNGECE_PowerAttackStaminaBuff_Tip	Power attacks cost this much less stamina.
$SNGECE_AttackSpeedBuff	Attack Speed Buff
$SNGECE_AttackSpeedBuff_Tip	Attack speed is increased by this amount. 0 turns the effect off (a workaround for mods with their own WeaponSpeedMult scaling).
$SNGECE_MoveSpeedBuff	Movement Speed Buff
$SNGECE_MoveSpeedBuff_Tip	Movement speed is increased by this many points.
$SNGECE_StaminaRegenBuff	Stamina Regeneration Buff
$SNGECE_StaminaRegenBuff_Tip	Stamina regenerates faster by this amount.
$SNGECE_AppliesOnClose	Values are saved and applied to the current stance when this page is closed.
$SNGECE_Header_Options	Extra Options
$SNGECE_RevertStance	Revert Stance
$SNGECE_RevertStance_Tip	Drop to Neutral stance while a bow, a crossbow or only spells and staves are equipped. The stance comes back when fists, a melee weapon or a shield are equipped again.
$SNGECE_RevertLock	Keep Neutral while reverted
$SNGECE_RevertLock_Tip	While the stance is reverted, switching to another stance is not possible. A stance picked in the meantime is applied once a melee weapon is back in hand.
$SNGECE_FixBaseAttackSpeed	Player Base Attack Speed
$SNGECE_FixBaseAttackSpeed_Tip	Attack speed changes need a base WeaponSpeedMult above 0. Most setups already have it from a fix mod (for example Attack Speed Framework); turn this on only if attack speed changes do nothing, otherwise attacks get twice as fast.
$SNGECE_HideStanceVisuals	Hide Stances NG switch effects
$SNGECE_HideStanceVisuals_Tip	No stance icon above the character and no shader on the body when switching. The stance is shown only by the indicator and the animation set.
$SNGECE_DebugLog	Debug log
$SNGECE_DebugLog_Tip	Writes stance changes, applied values and equipment checks to SKSE/StancesNGCombatExpansion.log. Turn on to report a problem.
$SNGECE_Header_Indicator	Stance Indicator
$SNGECE_ShowIndicator	Show stance indicator
$SNGECE_ShowIndicator_Tip	A small badge with the current stance next to the HUD. Hidden in menus and with the HUD turned off.
$SNGECE_IndicatorWeaponDrawn	Only with weapon drawn
$SNGECE_IndicatorWeaponDrawn_Tip	Hide the indicator while weapons are sheathed.
$SNGECE_IndicatorShowNeutral	Show in Neutral stance
$SNGECE_IndicatorShowNeutral_Tip	Keep the indicator visible when no stance is active.
$SNGECE_IndicatorScale	Indicator size
$SNGECE_IndicatorScale_Tip	Size relative to the default.
$SNGECE_IndicatorPosX	Horizontal position
$SNGECE_IndicatorPosX_Tip	Left edge of the indicator, percent of the screen width.
$SNGECE_IndicatorPosY	Vertical position
$SNGECE_IndicatorPosY_Tip	Bottom edge of the indicator, percent of the screen height from the top.
$SNGECE_Reset	Reset Combat Expansion Settings
$SNGECE_Reset_Tip	Restores the default bonuses and options. Indicator settings are kept.

$SNGECE_EnemyBonusHeader	Damage vs enemy types
$SNGECE_EnemyBonusNote	One bonus per enemy. An enemy of several types (a vampire is a person and undead) gets the largest of their bonuses; on a tie, the more specific type counts.
$SNGECE_EnemyBonus_Tip	Attack damage against this enemy type is higher by this amount in this stance. Works for enemies from other mods too.
